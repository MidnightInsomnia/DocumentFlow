# Evaluation artifact index

- `corpus-v1` — initial development/evaluation corpus.
- `corpus-v2-holdout` — historical holdout for strategies-v1; its corrected annotation is canonical.
- `corpus-v2-holdout` — development corpus for strategies-v2 after Research 4 analysis.
- `corpus-v3-final-holdout` — final untouched holdout for frozen strategies-v2.
- `holdout-results/v2-pre-erratum` — historical results only.
- `holdout-results/v2-corrected` — canonical corrected strategies-v1 holdout results.
- `development-results/v2-corrected` — canonical strategies-v2 development results.
- `final-results/v3` — official first-run final holdout results.

Nothing in the historical directories should be deleted or presented as a newer canonical result.
